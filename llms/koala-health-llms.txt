# Koala Health

> Koala Health is a digital pet pharmacy based in Boston, Massachusetts that dispenses prescription medications, vitamins, supplements and veterinary diets for dogs and cats direct to pet parents, with a focus on chronic-condition medication management. Medications ship in date- and time-sorted pouches with automatic refills, and prescriptions are fetched and verified directly with the pet's veterinarian. Koala also operates KoalaVet, a clinic-facing pharmacy platform that integrates with veterinary PIMS to sync client and patient data and let clinic teams review, approve and submit prescriptions.

Generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline on 2026-07-19 from the public Koala Health website. Koala Health publishes no public developer API, developer portal, API documentation or SDKs as of this date; `api.koala.health` is an internal Rails/Heroku administrative application behind sign-in.
